# Brief — w03 routing

**Topic:** Model routing (fast vs capable paths)
**Date researched:** 2026-11-11
**Corpus:** `content/source-corpus/latest.md`

## Summary

Routing sends easy tickets to cheaper/faster models and hard tickets to capable models. Acme uses heuristic routing after prompt chaining. Full gateway routing and caching land in w35.

## Contrarian angles

- Do not route before you can measure quality by path.
- Do not route on vibes — use length, intent=other, or explicit escalation tokens.
- Premium-everywhere is fine under low volume.
