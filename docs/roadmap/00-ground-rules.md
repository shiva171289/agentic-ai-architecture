# Learning in public — ground rules

Year 1 is **learning in public**: public architecture patterns, sample code, and articles. No employer use cases and no paid client work in Year 1.

## Content rules

1. **No employer material, ever.** No code, architecture, documents, customer names, internal tools, or problems you have only seen at work. Use fictional companies (for example **Acme Manufacturing**) and synthetic or public data.
2. **Generic patterns only.** Write about industry-standard patterns and public vendor services. Do not write “how we did X at my company”.
3. **Disclaimer** on the blog About page and in your LinkedIn About section:

   > Views and examples are my own, for learning purposes, and do not represent my employer.

4. **Personal resources only.** Personal time, personal GitHub (`shiva171289`), personal cloud accounts, and personal email. Do not use work devices or work hours for this project.
5. **Licensing.** MIT for code; CC BY 4.0 for written content (see repository root `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-content`).
6. **LinkedIn.** Agents never post to LinkedIn. You publish manually.
7. **Future paid work (Year 2+).** Before any paid engagement, re-read your employment contract moonlighting and IP clauses or ask HR. See [06-future-monetization.md](06-future-monetization.md).

## Agent enforcement

Every Hermes skill in `hermes/skills/` includes these rules. **ReviewerCritic** rejects drafts that mention real employers, internal tool names, or “at my company” phrasing.

## Device note

If your daily laptop is employer-managed, prefer a personal machine for this repo, or keep project files outside work sync paths and work only outside office hours.
