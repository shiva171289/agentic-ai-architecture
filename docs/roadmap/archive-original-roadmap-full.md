# Agentic AI Architecture — 5-year overview

**Profile:** Solutions Architect, 15 years · AWS & Azure · Chennai · October 2026 – October 2031

**Positioning:** Enterprise Agentic AI Architect | AWS and Azure | Learning in public

**Year 1 focus:** Skills, portfolio, and LinkedIn reach — not side income. Optional monetization starts Year 2+.

---

## Why this exists

Turn deep architecture experience into **public learning material**: weekly agentic patterns, runnable samples, AWS/Azure mappings, and a reusable framework. That builds industry standing and strengthens your day job (design reviews, multi-cloud choices, certifications).

Longer term, the same IP can support consulting, training, and products — see optional track in [docs/roadmap/06-future-monetization.md](docs/roadmap/06-future-monetization.md).

## Strategy (5 phases)

```text
Agentic AI Architecture → Audience & IP → (optional) Consulting → Training → Products / SaaS
```

## Five-year phases (summary)

| Phase | Period | Focus |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Year 1** | Oct 2026 – Oct 2027 | Learning in public: ~48 patterns, 5 flagships, framework v1, certifications |
| **Year 2** | Oct 2027 – Oct 2028 | Optional: productized architecture reviews & workshops |
| **Year 3** | Oct 2028 – Oct 2029 | Optional: course + digital products |
| **Year 4** | Oct 2029 – Oct 2030 | Optional: small delivery network + validated product idea |
| **Year 5** | Oct 2030 – Oct 2031 | Optional: scale recurring revenue mix |

Income targets and service pricing live only in the optional monetization doc.

## Year 1 operating model

- **Weekly:** 1 pattern → blog + 2 LinkedIn posts + sample code + diagrams + AWS/Azure mapping
- **Agents:** Hermes on Hostinger VPS, controlled via Telegram (~$10/mo OpenRouter)
- **You:** Approve ADRs, edit voice, polish in Cursor, publish manually
- **Time:** ~8 hours/week
